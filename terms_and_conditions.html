Daily MCQ Challenge & Leaderboard Rules

To ensure a transparent, motivating, and fair learning competition for all students, participation in the Daily MCQ Challenge is governed by the following rules:

  • Single Official Attempt Per Day: Each student is permitted one (1) official Daily Test attempt per calendar day (IST). Once started or submitted, the official attempt is locked for that calendar day across all courses.
  • Ranking Methodology: Leaderboard positions are calculated first by highest marks scored, and in the event of a tie, by fastest completion time (in seconds).
  • Midnight IST Reset: Every night at 12:00:00 AM IST, the Daily Test and Daily Leaderboard reset automatically for the new calendar day.
  • Display Name Conduct: Students must use respectful display names. The use of abusive, offensive, misleading, or impersonating names on the public leaderboard is strictly prohibited and such entries may be removed without notice.
  • Anti-Cheating Policy: Any attempt to manipulate test timers, submit falsified scores through automated scripts, or tamper with network requests will result in disqualification from the leaderboard.

Practice Mode, Exam Mode & Scoring

  • Practice Mode: Designed for self-paced learning with instant option verification, detailed explanations, and bilingual Voice Reading (Text-to-Speech) support.

  • Negative Marking: Certain tests carry negative marking for incorrect answers as displayed on the Test Instructions screen prior to starting the test. Unattempted questions carry zero deduction.
